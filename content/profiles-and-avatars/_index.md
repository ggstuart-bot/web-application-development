In this unit we will give every user a profile page, with a picture of their own.
Shelves are worth sharing, so we will show everyone's shelves, and say whose they are.

<!--more-->

First we will list everyone's shelves on the home page, link each shelf to its owner, and give each user a profile page that lists their shelves.
Then we will store pictures in the database, show them beside users' names, and let each user upload their own.
Along the way we will meet file uploads, and use the permissions pattern from the last unit again.
