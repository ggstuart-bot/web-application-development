In this unit we will finish our privacy rules, and give some users the power to look after the others.

<!--more-->

First we will make sure that a public shelf always has a public owner, and meet our first `403 Forbidden`, for something you can see but aren't allowed to do.
Then we will add admins, with a users page that only they can open, and let them make other users admins, and take it away again.
This is the last unit of the bookshelves app, and it completes the permissions pattern we have been building since [owners and guards][pattern].
