In this unit we will give books and shelves pages of their own, and link them together.
Every book and every shelf becomes a *resource*, with its own address.

<!--more-->

First we will give each book a page, and teach our router to read the book's id from the path.
Then we will add shelves, with a table, some seed data, a page that lists them and a page for each one.
Finally we will put books on shelves, so each shelf page shows its books and each shelf card shows a few covers.
Everything in this unit uses links and `GET` requests.
