In this unit we will decide who is allowed to do what.
Signing in tells us who someone is, and now we can act on it.

<!--more-->

First we will guard the routes that change shelves, so only signed-in users can use them, and send signed-in users away from the sign up and sign in pages.
Then we will give every shelf an owner.
Each user gets their own shelves, other people's shelves are shown without their buttons, and only a shelf's owner can change it.
Along the way we will meet a pattern for permissions that we will use for the rest of the module.
